🎯 Objective

Deflect the bouncing ball with your paddle to smash all colored bricks on screen while keeping the ball in play.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Slide your paddle left and right. Bounce the ball into the brick wall. Angle your paddle strikes to send the ball into hard-to-reach pockets. Catch power-ups for laser and wide paddle!

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This arcade physics game measures paddle angle control and ball rebound anticipation. It tests your ability to aim ball deflections based on where the ball strikes your paddle surface.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Hit the ball near the outer edges of your paddle to produce sharp, high-angle ricochet shots.
  • 2. Strike near the paddle center to return a safe, predictable vertical rebound.
  • 3. Tunnel the ball behind the top brick layer to let it ricochet repeatedly against the ceiling.
  • 4. Catch falling power-ups like multi-ball and wide paddle, but steer clear of speed-boost hazards.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do paddle hit zones affect ball trajectory in Paddle Brick Buster?

The paddle is divided into five reflection zones: edge strikes create steep 60° angles, while center strikes reflect vertically.

How many lives do you start with?

You begin with three paddle lives; losing all lives ends the run, while clearing all bricks advances to the next stage.

Can you control the paddle with both mouse and keyboard?

Yes, mouse dragging provides smooth analog tracking, while Left/Right arrow keys offer consistent digital stepping.
