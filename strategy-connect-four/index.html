Drop discs into the 7-column vertical grid to connect 4 of your color before the bot does.

🎯 Objective

Drop colored discs into the vertical grid to form an unbroken line of four pieces while preventing your opponent from doing so.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Tap any column to drop a disc. Discs fall to the lowest unoccupied space. The first player to connect four discs in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) wins!

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This game measures vertical gravity column planning and parity trap creation. It tests how you control central columns and avoid giving your opponent setup moves.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Control the center column (column 4); central discs participate in the highest number of potential winning lines.
  • 2. Never drop a disc directly beneath your opponent's potential fourth winning square unless forced to.
  • 3. Build multiple overlapping threats on both horizontal and diagonal planes simultaneously.
  • 4. Keep track of odd versus even column parity when playing as player one or player two.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the grid size in Four in a Line?

The standard board contains 7 vertical columns and 6 horizontal rows for a total of 42 playable slots.

Can winning lines be formed diagonally?

Yes, lines of four can be formed horizontally, vertically, or along either diagonal orientation.

Who moves first in match play?

Player one moves first with yellow discs, followed by the computer opponent playing red discs.
