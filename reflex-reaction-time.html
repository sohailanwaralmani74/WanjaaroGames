🎯 Objective

Click or tap the screen the moment the red background turns solid green.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Tap or click to arm the sensor. Wait patiently while the screen is red. As soon as it flashes green, tap as fast as humanly possible. Tapping too early incurs a false-start penalty.

Scored in ms (lower values indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This test measures your simple visual response latency in milliseconds. It tracks the time between when the screen color changes and when your input device registers your click.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Rest your finger gently on the mouse button or touchscreen before the light turns green.
  • 2. Fix your gaze directly on the center of the screen to catch the color shift immediately.
  • 3. Avoid anticipating the color change, as clicking early counts as a false start.
  • 4. Take three deep breaths before each trial to keep your forearm muscles relaxed.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered a normal reaction time on this test?

Most healthy adults score between 215 and 250 milliseconds using a desktop mouse or responsive touchscreen.

Why does tapping too early reset the round?

False starts are penalized because true reaction time measures response to a stimulus, not random guessing.

Does screen refresh rate affect my score?

Yes, displays running at 144Hz or higher update the visual frame faster than older 60Hz screens, reducing input display delay by roughly 10 milliseconds.
