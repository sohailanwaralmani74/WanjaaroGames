🎯 Objective

Hold your cursor or stylus steadily inside a moving micro-circle without touching its perimeter boundaries.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Click and drag the cursor ring along the winding path from Start to Goal. If your cursor drifts off the path or touches the boundary wire, the circuit buzzes and resets!

Scored in sec (lower values indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This test measures fine motor stabilization and low-amplitude tracking accuracy. It records involuntary hand tremor and micro-adjustment control over sustained tracking paths.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Grip your mouse or stylus lightly without clenching your fingers to minimize muscle tremor.
  • 2. Rest your forearm comfortably on the desk to provide a stable mechanical pivot point.
  • 3. Track the center point of the circle rather than watching its boundaries.
  • 4. Use slow, continuous wrist sweeps rather than jerky micro-corrections.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the game track steady holding?

The engine samples cursor coordinates every millisecond and deducts stability points whenever the pointer leaves the target zone.

Does pointer acceleration interfere with Micro Steady Hand?

Yes, turning off "Enhance Pointer Precision" in your operating system provides true 1:1 hardware tracking.

How long does each tracking trial last?

Standard trials run for fifteen seconds of continuous movement across winding paths.
