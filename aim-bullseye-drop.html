🎯 Objective

Release falling supply crates so they land directly on moving conveyor target carts below.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

A basket oscillates horizontally at the bottom. Tap the Release button at the exact moment so the falling sphere lands directly in the basket center.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This game measures vertical gravity lead calculation and intercept trajectory timing. It tests your judgment of falling acceleration against moving horizontal ground targets.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Account for downward gravitational acceleration; crates drop faster the longer they fall.
  • 2. Release the crate when the target cart is roughly one crate-width before the drop line.
  • 3. Notice variations in cart speeds and adjust your lead distance accordingly.
  • 4. Avoid dropping crates when carts are turning around at track edges.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does crate weight vary between rounds?

Standard crates have uniform mass, but later bonus rounds introduce heavy golden containers that fall faster.

How are bullseye accuracy points scored?

Direct center hits earn 100 points, with partial edge landings awarding 25 to 50 points based on offset distance.

What happens if a crate misses the cart entirely?

A dropped crate that hits the floor breaks your combo multiplier and loses one life.
