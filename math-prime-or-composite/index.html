🎯 Objective

Classify flashed whole numbers as either Prime or Composite as quickly and accurately as possible.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

A number between 2 and 150 appears. Hit PRIME if it has no divisors other than 1 and itself; hit COMPOSITE if it has factors. Win points for speed and accuracy.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This math game measures divisibility rule application and number theory categorization. It tests your speed at checking small factor multiples (2, 3, 5, 7, 11).

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Any even number greater than 2 is immediately composite.
  • 2. Use the digit-sum rule for 3: if the sum of all digits is divisible by 3, the number is composite.
  • 3. Any number ending in 5 or 0 (other than 5 itself) is composite.
  • 4. Memorize tricky small primes under 100 like 53, 59, 61, 71, 73, 79, 83, 89, and 97.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is the number 1 considered a prime number?

No, in mathematics 1 is neither prime nor composite; it has only one positive divisor.

Is the number 2 a prime number?

Yes, 2 is the smallest prime number and the only even prime number in existence.

What is the highest number tested in Prime Detective?

Standard mode tests numbers up to 150, with master stages testing three-digit numbers up to 500.
