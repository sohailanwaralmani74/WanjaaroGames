🎯 Objective

Locate and click numbers 1 through 25 on the 5x5 matrix in strict ascending numerical order as fast as possible.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

A 5x5 grid containing numbers 1 to 25 scrambled randomly. Click number 1, then 2, then 3, all the way to 25. Used by cosmonauts and speed-readers to train peripheral vision.

Scored in sec (lower values indicate better performance).

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Keep your eyes fixed on the center square (number 13) and use peripheral vision to locate numbers.
  • 2. Avoid moving your head or tracing your finger across the screen; move only your eyes and mouse.
  • 3. As you click one number, begin searching for the subsequent number before your hand returns.
  • 4. Notice where numbers in the next tier (like 6-10 while clicking 1-5) are located to prepare your route.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Schulte Table?

Developed by German psychiatrist Walter Schulte, it is a standard psychological test used to measure visual search velocity and attention distribution.

What is considered a fast completion time for a 5x5 Schulte Table?

The average adult completes a 25-number grid in 32 to 45 seconds; speed readers and trained esports players finish in under 20 seconds.

What happens if you click a number out of order?

The grid flashes red and refuses to advance until the correct next sequential number is clicked.
