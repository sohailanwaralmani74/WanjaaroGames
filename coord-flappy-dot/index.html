🎯 Objective

Tap to flap your aerodynamic pulse upward through narrow gate clearances while gravity pulls you downward.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Press Space or tap screen to give your dot an upward aerodynamic impulse. Let gravity pull you downward. Thread smoothly through gaps in scrolling obstacles.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This arcade game measures vertical impulse frequency modulation and spatial clearance judgment. It benchmarks your ability to sustain level flight through alternating gravity and impulse pulses.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Tap in small, measured pulses rather than letting the dot drop far before double-tapping.
  • 2. Aim to enter gates near their vertical center to maximize error tolerance on both ceiling and floor.
  • 3. Tap slightly before reaching a gate if you need to rise through an elevated opening.
  • 4. Keep a calm, relaxed tapping rhythm to prevent over-flapping into ceiling spikes.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does gravity acceleration work in Aero Pulse Flap?

Gravity pulls downward with constant downward acceleration, while each tap imparts a fixed upward velocity burst.

Do gate openings become narrower as your score increases?

Gate vertical gaps narrow gradually from 120 pixels down to 75 pixels after score milestones at 10 and 25 gates.

Is there any recovery if you graze a pipe gate?

Any contact with gate edges or floor boundaries ends the run immediately, recording your highest gate count.
