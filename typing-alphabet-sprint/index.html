🎯 Objective

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Press Start, then type A, B, C... through Z without making a single mistake. The clock stops the instant you hit Z. Elite typists achieve sub-2.0 seconds!

🧠 What This Game Measures

This sprint measures full-keyboard finger travel time and sequential alphabetic motor memory. It records your millisecond execution time across the entire keyboard layout.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Memorize your finger assignments for tricky letters like Q, X, and Z in advance.
  • 2. Keep your typing pressure light so your hands float effortlessly across keyboard rows.
  • 3. Practice transitioning smoothly between the left and right hands (e.g. from B to C to D).
  • 4. Maintain an unbroken rhythmic cadence rather than pausing between letters.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a competitive time for typing the alphabet A to Z?

Most everyday typists finish in 4 to 6 seconds; elite keyboard sprinters complete the full alphabet in under 2 seconds.

What happens if you type an incorrect letter?

The timer continues running while the screen flashes red until the correct consecutive letter is entered.

Can you use a mechanical or laptop keyboard?

Any standard QWERTY, Dvorak, or Colemak physical keyboard is supported, with low-profile keys often enabling faster finger glides.
