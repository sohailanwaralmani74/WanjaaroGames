🎯 Objective

Pilot your starship through a dense oncoming asteroid field, dodging space rocks and gathering cosmic fuel pods.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Use touch or arrow keys to steer your ship. Weave through incoming space debris. Collect blue fuel crystals to keep your shields powered up. Survive as long as you can.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This space arcade game measures 2D evasion control and hazard trajectory prediction. It records your survival duration under increasing obstacle density and velocity.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Stay near the vertical center of the arena rather than hugging screen borders where you can be cornered.
  • 2. Watch for warning flash indicators along screen edges signaling incoming high-speed meteorites.
  • 3. Gather golden shield orbs to gain temporary collision invulnerability.
  • 4. Make small vertical adjustments to slide between parallel asteroid trajectories.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do asteroids break apart when destroyed?

Large asteroids shatter into two smaller, faster fragments when blasted with collected plasma torpedoes.

How is your score calculated in Asteroid Field Navigator?

Your score combines total survival time in seconds with bonus points earned from collected energy fuel cells.

Can you shoot asteroids or only dodge them?

Core gameplay focuses on evasive steering, with temporary plasma blaster power-ups available in later waves.
