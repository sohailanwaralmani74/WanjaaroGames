🎯 Objective

Connect your two opposite board edges with an unbroken chain of adjacent colored hexagonal tiles.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

You start at the top-left hex. Choose one of 5 colors. All adjacent hexes of that color are absorbed into your growing territory. Conquer more than 50% of the board before moves expire.

Scored in moves (lower values indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This connection game measures topology connectivity planning and path blocking. It benchmarks your construction of un-blockable virtual connections and bridge templates.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Use two-space "bridge" configurations; if your opponent enters one open gap, you immediately take the other.
  • 2. Balance offensive path progression with defensive blocking moves across opposing diagonal corridors.
  • 3. Control central hexagonal tiles early to maximize your branching route choices.
  • 4. Remember that draws are mathematically impossible in Hex; one player must always form a connection.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can a match of Hex ever end in a draw?

No, John Nash mathematically proved that the game of Hex can never end in a draw; one player will always complete a path.

Which edges belong to which player?

Player one connects North to South with gold tiles, while player two connects West to East with blue tiles.

What is a "bridge" in Hex strategy?

A bridge consists of two stones separated by two shared empty hexes, guaranteeing connection because the opponent cannot block both.
