🎯 Objective

Guide your spark continuously through an accelerating storm of blue kinetic particles without getting hit.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Move your mouse cursor or drag your finger to pilot the golden spark. Avoid all converging blue energy particles. Survive as long as you can while velocity escalates.

Scored in sec (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This test measures continuous evasion control and spatial trajectory anticipation. It records how many seconds you can sustain collision-free movement under increasing hazard density.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Stay near the central area of the arena to leave yourself escape routes in all directions.
  • 2. Make small, controlled movements rather than sweeping panicky strokes across the screen.
  • 3. Avoid backing yourself into corners where converging particles can trap you.
  • 4. Watch the trajectories of incoming particles several steps ahead rather than staring only at your own spark.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do the hazard particles move in random directions?

Particles bounce off boundaries according to kinetic reflection angles and slowly increase in velocity.

Is mouse control or touch control recommended for Dodge Vector?

Both work well, but a low-friction mouse on desktop provides precise micro-adjustments with zero hand occlusion.

How is survival time measured?

Your score tracks exact elapsed time from your first movement until particle contact.
