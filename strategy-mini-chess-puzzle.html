💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Identify all flight squares around the defending king before calculating checks.
  • 2. Look for double checks delivered by moving an attacking piece that also unmasks a rear line check.
  • 3. Check whether the defending king has friendly pieces blocking its potential escape squares.
  • 4. Ensure the checking piece cannot be captured by any enemy piece on the board.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What chess rating corresponds to these mate-in-one puzzles?

Puzzles range from beginner patterns (500–800 Elo) to subtle quiet checkmates (1000–1200 Elo).

What happens if you play a check that is not checkmate?

Any move that does not deliver immediate mate-in-one is marked incorrect and restarts the puzzle timer.

Can you castle or promote pawns in these puzzles?

Yes, select endgame positions require pawn promotion to Queen or Knight to deliver checkmate.
