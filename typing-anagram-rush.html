🎯 Objective

Rearrange the scrambled letter tiles to form as many valid dictionary words as possible before the timer runs out.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

A scrambled anagram appears (e.g. "T R E A H" -> "EARTH"). Type the correct unscrambled English word and press Enter. Solve as many anagrams as you can in 60 seconds.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This word puzzle measures anagram vocabulary parsing and lexical flexibility. It benchmarks your speed at unscrambling letter groups into correct words.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Look for common prefixes (un-, re-, pre-) and suffixes (-ed, -ing, -er) among the scrambled letters.
  • 2. Rearrange vowels and consonants into alternating patterns to spark word recognition.
  • 3. Submit shorter 3-letter and 4-letter words early to build momentum and banked time.
  • 4. Shuffle the letters using the spacebar if you get stuck on a difficult rack.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What dictionary is used to validate submitted anagrams?

Submissions are verified against standard Scrabble and tournament word lists (TWL06 / CSW).

Do longer words award bonus points in Anagram Rush?

Yes, points scale exponentially: a 6-letter word awards four times more points than two 3-letter words.

Can letters be reused more than once per word?

You can only use each letter tile as many times as it appears in the scrambled rack for that round.
