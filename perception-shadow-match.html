🎯 Objective

Match the 3D rendered item with its exact corresponding 2D silhouette cast on the wall.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

A rotating 3D polygon is presented in perspective. Choose the exact 2D projection shadow it would cast onto the floor from the four candidate silhouettes.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This puzzle measures dimensional projection analysis and silhouette contour matching. It evaluates how accurately you project a complex object outline onto a flat 2D shadow profile.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Identify unique protruding features (like handles, spikes, or corners) and search for them on the silhouette.
  • 2. Check negative space openings inside the object that should appear as cutouts in the shadow.
  • 3. Beware of decoy silhouettes that differ only by a single reversed handle or flipped orientation.
  • 4. Rotate the 3D model with your mouse if needed to align it with the shadow casting angle.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can silhouettes be rotated relative to the original model?

Silhouettes reflect light cast from a fixed point source, maintaining a consistent projection angle.

How many shadow choices are provided per challenge?

Each round presents four silhouette candidates, with three containing subtle structural distortions.

What is the best technique for complex organic shapes?

Count the number of major bumps or contours along the outer perimeter to quickly eliminate false shadows.
