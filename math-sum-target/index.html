🎯 Objective

Select a subset of numbers from the grid whose combined sum equals the designated target value.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

A target sum (e.g. 27) is shown alongside a grid of single and double-digit integers. Select the combination of numbers that sums exactly to the target. Multiple solutions may exist!

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This puzzle measures subset sum combinatorial arithmetic and addition grouping. It tests your ability to filter through number sets to find exact sum combinations under time constraints.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Look for numbers with units digits that add up to the units digit of your target.
  • 2. Pair large numbers with small numbers to stay close to the target range without overshooting.
  • 3. Exclude numbers that are strictly larger than the requested target sum immediately.
  • 4. Look for clean tens pairs (like 3 + 7 = 10, or 4 + 6 = 10) to make mental math faster.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can you select any quantity of numbers to reach the sum?

Yes, unless a stage explicitly specifies a 2-card or 3-card constraint, any combination that equals the target is valid.

What grid size is used in Sum Target Matrix?

Numbers are arranged on a 3x3 or 4x4 matrix containing values between 1 and 50.

Is there always at least one valid combination on the board?

Yes, boards are generated with guaranteed valid subsets that equal the target value.
