🎯 Objective

Spot and click the hidden target glyph concealed within a noisy background texture or pattern.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Search through a dense field of repeating geometric distractors to find the single unique symbol. Tap it as fast as possible to progress to harder stages.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This test measures visual figure-ground segregation and pattern extraction. It benchmarks how quickly you isolate a target shape embedded inside visual noise.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Scan along horizontal bands across the texture rather than wandering randomly.
  • 2. Look for unnatural line intersections or continuous curves that disrupt the background grain.
  • 3. Adjust your distance from the monitor slightly; sitting back often makes embedded shapes stand out.
  • 4. Notice symmetry clues, as target glyphs usually possess geometric balance absent in the noise.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does the background camouflage change each round?

Yes, patterns alternate between geometric hatches, organic noise grains, and stippled dot fields.

What happens if you click the wrong area of the camouflage?

Misclicks incur a five-second penalty added to your total search time for that stage.

Is there a hint button if a glyph is impossible to find?

A hint button is available after 15 seconds, highlighting the general quadrant of the hidden symbol at the cost of score stars.
