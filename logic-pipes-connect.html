🎯 Objective

Rotate pipe segments across the grid to establish an unbroken, leak-free conduit from source valve to drain exit.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Tap any pipe segment to rotate it 90 degrees clockwise. Align all conduits so water can flow in an unbroken path from the green inlet valve to the gold outlet reservoir.

Scored in moves (lower values indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This puzzle measures network topology visualization and path continuity planning. It evaluates how you align rotational connections so flow travels without dead ends.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Start connecting from the fixed source valve and the fixed destination drain first.
  • 2. Examine corner pipes along outer grid walls, as their orientations are strictly limited by boundaries.
  • 3. Ensure every branch of T-junctions feeds into a valid connector rather than hitting a blank wall.
  • 4. Double check all loop closures before turning the master water valve on.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when water is released into the pipe network?

Water flows through all valid connected segments. If it reaches an open end, it leaks and docks points.

Can there be unused pipe segments left over on the grid?

Some complex puzzle levels include decorative dead-end pipe tiles that do not need to connect to the main line.

How do you rotate a pipe segment?

Click or tap any pipe tile to rotate it 90 degrees clockwise; double-click rotates 180 degrees.
