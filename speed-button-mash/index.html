🎯 Objective

Mash two alternating keys (A and B) in perfect sequential cadence to drive the speedometer needle into the maximum zone.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Alternate hitting button A then B as fast as humanly possible for 10 seconds. Pressing the same button twice in a row incurs an invalid stroke penalty.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This drill measures reciprocal dual-key motor cadence and endurance stamina. It evaluates your ability to alternate keystrokes rapidly without double-pressing the same key.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Use the index fingers of separate hands (one on A, one on B) for maximum alternating speed.
  • 2. Establish a strict 1-2-1-2 rhythmic tempo; pressing the same key twice in succession breaks your momentum.
  • 3. Keep your keyboard switches clean and free of sticky residue for crisp actuation rebound.
  • 4. Start at a sustainable cadence and accelerate during the final three seconds of the sprint.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which keyboard keys are used for Alternating Mash?

Standard desktop controls use the Left and Right arrow keys or the A and D keys on your keyboard.

What is the penalty for pressing the same key twice?

Double-pressing the same key locks the gauge for 200 milliseconds, stalling your speedometer needle.

What is a competitive score on this drill?

Reaching 80 to 100 alternating cycles within 10 seconds places you in the top tier of competitive mashers.
