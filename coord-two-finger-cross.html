🎯 Objective

Synchronize two fingers to tap alternating left and right screen triggers in precise counter-phase timing.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Place one finger / mouse on the Left Dot and one on the Right Dot. Guide both through their respective security gates when both gates open simultaneously.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This drill measures bimanual phase alternation and reciprocal tapping cadence. It benchmarks your ability to maintain a rapid, balanced 180-degree phase shift between two hands.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Think of your fingers as walking legs: left, right, left, right in an unbroken gait.
  • 2. Tap with equal force on both sides to prevent one hand from dominating the cadence.
  • 3. Keep your finger lifts low to minimize travel time between alternating strokes.
  • 4. Focus on the metronome beat indicator to prevent tempo drift.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can you use a keyboard for Twin Gate Crossing?

Yes, keyboard controls use the Z key for left gate and the M key for right gate.

What is a phase error in this game?

A phase error occurs if both fingers tap simultaneously or if one finger taps twice in succession.

How long does a standard speed trial last?

Standard sessions run for 20 seconds of continuous alternating tapping.
