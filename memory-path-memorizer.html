🎯 Objective

Watch a golden path illuminate through the dark tile maze, then retrace the exact route from memory.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Observe the bright trail moving from start to finish. When prompted, swipe or click adjacent cells to reproduce the exact route taken without making wrong turns.

Scored in streak (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This game measures spatial directional route encoding. It benchmarks how many sequential coordinate steps of a winding labyrinth you can commit to memory and retrace.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Mentally call out directions (up, up, right, down, right) as the path draws across the floor.
  • 2. Visualize the path as an overall letter or glyph shape instead of isolated individual squares.
  • 3. Pay close attention to directional turn points rather than straight corridor segments.
  • 4. Do not rush; pause for a moment to verify your next step before clicking a tile.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if you step onto a tile off the path?

Stepping off the route turns the tile red and costs one life out of three total attempts.

Can the path cross over itself?

No, paths never overlap or cross previous footsteps, maintaining a continuous single thread.

How long do you have to examine the illuminated path before it vanishes?

The demonstration displays each step for 400 milliseconds, followed by a one-second pause before player control.
