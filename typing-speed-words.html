💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Focus on error-free typing first; rhythm and speed naturally follow from high accuracy.
  • 2. Read one or two words ahead of what your fingers are actively typing.
  • 3. Keep your wrists elevated slightly above the desk to prevent dragging fatigue.
  • 4. Use all ten fingers on their standard home-row positions rather than hunting and pecking.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is net words per minute (WPM) calculated?

One word is standardized as five keystrokes. Net WPM is calculated as (Gross Words - Uncorrected Errors) divided by elapsed minutes.

What is considered a good typing speed for general users?

The average adult types between 40 and 55 WPM, while professional transcriptionists and programmers often reach 80 to 100+ WPM.

Does the test penalize backspacing to correct typos?

You can backspace to fix errors before pressing space, but fixing mistakes consumes valuable sprint seconds.
