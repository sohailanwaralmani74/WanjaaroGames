🎯 Objective

Toggle individual bit switches (0 and 1) along the byte row to match the requested decimal integer target.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

A target decimal number between 0 and 255 is displayed. Click the binary bit toggles (0 or 1) to make the sum of active power-of-two bits equal the target as fast as possible.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This drill measures binary-to-decimal base conversion and powers-of-two mental arithmetic. It tests your fluency with binary place values (128, 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1).

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Compare your target number to 128; if greater or equal, toggle the 128 bit on and subtract 128 from your running sum.
  • 2. Continue down the powers of two (64, 32, 16, etc.), toggling bits that fit into your remaining balance.
  • 3. Remember that odd decimal numbers always require the final 1-bit to be turned on.
  • 4. Familiarize yourself with power-of-two additions to calculate 8-bit bytes rapidly.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum decimal number you can build with an 8-bit byte?

An 8-bit byte with all bits toggled to 1 equals 255 (128 + 64 + 32 + 16 + 8 + 4 + 2 + 1).

Does the game support reverse mode (binary to decimal)?

Yes, an alternating mode displays active binary switches and asks you to enter the equivalent decimal sum.

How much time do you get per conversion problem?

Sprint mode gives you 15 seconds per conversion, with speed bonuses for answers submitted under 5 seconds.
