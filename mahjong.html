🎯 Objective

Match and clear open pairs of identical traditional Chinese tiles until the entire layered pyramid board is cleared.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Select and match pairs of identical free tiles to clear the board. A tile is free if it has no tiles above it and has at least one open side (left or right).

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Focus on clearing tall vertical stacks and long horizontal rows first, as they trap the highest number of hidden tiles.
  • 2. Never match a pair immediately if doing so leaves other critical rows blocked; survey all candidate matches.
  • 3. Remember that Season tiles match with any Season, and Flower tiles match with any Flower.
  • 4. Keep track of tile quadruplets: if three matching tiles are visible, matching two of them frees the remaining one.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a tile "free" to match in Mahjong Solitaire?

A tile is free if: 1) No other tile rests directly on top of it, and 2) Either its left edge or right edge is completely open with no touching neighbor.

How many total tiles are in a classic Mahjong Solitaire set?

A standard game features 144 tiles: three suits (Dots, Bamboos, Characters) from 1 to 9, four Winds, three Dragons, four Seasons, and four Flowers.
