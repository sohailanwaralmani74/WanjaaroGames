🎯 Objective

Estimate the degree angle of the displayed geometric vertex and dial in your closest guess (0° to 180°).

📋 How to Play & Scoring

An acute, right, or obtuse angle is drawn. Enter your estimate in degrees. Scores are awarded based on how close your estimate is to the mathematical truth (within 5° is a bullseye!).

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This game measures geometric angle estimation and spatial slope perception. It benchmarks your precision at gauging angular degrees without physical measurement tools.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Anchor your estimate against standard landmarks: 45° (half right), 90° (perpendicular), and 135°.
  • 2. Visualize a full 90-degree square corner in the vertex to see whether the angle is acute or obtuse.
  • 3. Use the circular slider smoothly to fine-tune your degree guess before hitting submit.
  • 4. Look at the arc between the two rays rather than focusing on ray lengths.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How close does my estimate need to be for a perfect score?

Guesses within plus-or-minus two degrees receive a 100% precision rating and maximum bonus points.

Do ray lengths vary between problems?

Yes, rays have unequal lengths and orientations to ensure you judge angle slope rather than line endpoints.

Can angles exceed 180 degrees in this drill?

Standard mode focuses on interior angles between 5° and 175°, with an advanced mode testing reflex angles up to 355°.
