🎯 Objective

Combine four dealt number cards using basic arithmetic (+, -, ×, ÷) to build an equation equal to exactly 24.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Four numbers are drawn (e.g. 3, 3, 8, 8). Combine them using basic arithmetic operations so the final result is 24 (e.g. 8 / (3 - 8/3) = 24). Tap numbers and signs in sequence.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This game measures arithmetic factor exploration and flexible algebraic manipulation. It tests your ability to spot factors of 24 (like 3×8, 4×6, 2×12, or 24/1) among arbitrary digit sets.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Look for ways to form classic factors of 24: such as 3 and 8, or 4 and 6, or 2 and 12.
  • 2. If you have a 3 and an 8, see if your remaining two cards can be combined to form 1 (e.g. 7 - 6 = 1).
  • 3. Look for fractions that multiply out, such as 8 / (1 - 1/3) = 24 on advanced puzzles.
  • 4. Try making numbers that subtract or add to 24 (such as 20 + 4, or 27 - 3).
