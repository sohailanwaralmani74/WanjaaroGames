🎯 Objective

Rotate and fit all geometric tans into the target silhouette without any pieces overlapping or sticking out.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Drag and position geometric polygon pieces into the outlined target silhouette. All pieces must fit without overlapping or protruding past the boundary lines.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This geometric puzzle measures shape decomposition and polygon assembly. It tests how you partition a solid silhouette into constituent triangles, squares, and parallelograms.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Place the two largest triangles first; they consume the majority of the silhouette area.
  • 2. Look for 90-degree corners in the target silhouette to anchor the square and medium triangle.
  • 3. Remember that the parallelogram can be flipped horizontally to change its slant orientation.
  • 4. Rotate pieces in 45-degree increments to test fit against straight boundary edges.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are the seven traditional tangram pieces (tans)?

A classic tangram set consists of two large triangles, one medium triangle, two small triangles, one square, and one parallelogram.

Can pieces overlap each other on the board?

No, all seven pieces must lay flat edge-to-edge within the silhouette with zero overlapping boundaries.

How do you rotate a piece on desktop and mobile?

Click or tap a selected piece to rotate it in 45-degree steps, or use dedicated rotation buttons on mobile.
