## Overview

Rapid-fire single and double-digit multiplication drill: answer 20 products in record time.

## Instructions

Products from the 2x2 through 15x15 times tables are presented. Enter the product immediately. A clean streak with zero errors provides massive score bonuses.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Multiply by 9 by multiplying by 10 and subtracting the number once (e.g. 9 x 7 = 70 - 7 = 63).

## Underlying Mechanics

Multiplication table retrieval drill with stopwatch benchmark.
