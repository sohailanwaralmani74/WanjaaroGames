## Overview

Aim the bubble cannon to launch colored bubbles and clear clusters of 3 or more.

## Instructions

Aim the pointer and tap to fire the loaded colored bubble. Connect 3 or more bubbles of the same color to burst them. Prevent the bubble ceiling from lowering to the ground.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Bounce shots off the side walls to reach high anchor bubbles and trigger massive ceiling drops.

## Underlying Mechanics

Hexagonal grid bubble raycast targeting with connected component flood removal.
