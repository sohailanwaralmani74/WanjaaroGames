## Overview

A swarm of dots jiggles randomly. Spot the subset drifting in a unified direction.

## Instructions

In a swarm of 100 jittery dots, 15% move coherently in one cardinal direction (Up, Down, Left, or Right). Detect and click the direction of coherent drift.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Step back slightly from your screen. The macroscopic flow of movement becomes clearer at a distance.

## Underlying Mechanics

Random dot kinematogram (RDK) visual cortex motion coherence threshold tester.
