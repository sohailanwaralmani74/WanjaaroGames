## Overview

Shrinking crosshair targets appear across the firing range. Hit dead center.

## Instructions

Click or tap moving bullseyes before they collapse to zero size. Hitting closer to the dead center yields maximum points (up to 100 per target). Complete 15 targets.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Take an extra 50ms to verify your cursor is dead center rather than rushing an edge hit.

## Underlying Mechanics

Euclidean distance radial scoring engine with shrinking target geometry.
