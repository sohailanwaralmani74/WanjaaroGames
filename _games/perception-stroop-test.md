## Overview

Choose the font ink color, while ignoring what the conflicting word says.

## Instructions

A color name appears (e.g. the word "BLUE" written in RED ink). Select the button that matches the INK COLOR (Red), not the word text! A premier test of cognitive conflict resolution.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Blur your vision slightly so you register the raw photons of color without reading the semantic word.

## Underlying Mechanics

Classic neuropsychological Stroop interference engine with congruous/incongruous distribution.
