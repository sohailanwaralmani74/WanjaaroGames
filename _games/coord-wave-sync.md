## Overview

Adjust amplitude and frequency sliders to superimpose your sine wave onto the reference wave.

## Instructions

A target sine wave oscillates on the oscilloscope. Drag Amplitude and Frequency sliders until your active wave perfectly overlays the target waveform.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Match the peak height (Amplitude) first, then adjust the horizontal spacing (Frequency).

## Underlying Mechanics

Trigonometric sine wave function evaluation with root-mean-square difference error calculation.
