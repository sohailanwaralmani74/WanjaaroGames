## Overview

Inspect two intersecting rays and guess the exact angle from 0° to 180° within 5 degrees.

## Instructions

An acute, right, or obtuse angle is drawn. Enter your estimate in degrees. Scores are awarded based on how close your estimate is to the mathematical truth (within 5° is a bullseye!).

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Anchor against standard benchmarks: 45° is half of 90°, 135° is halfway between 90° and 180°.

## Underlying Mechanics

Ray angle generator with Euclidean arc measurement comparison.
