## Overview

Keep a rolling metal ball balanced on a tilting pivot board as long as you can.

## Instructions

Use Left/Right keys or tap sides of the screen to tilt the seesaw platform. Counteract gravity and momentum to keep the ball from rolling off the platform edges.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Make tiny counter-tilts early rather than waiting for the ball to pick up high momentum.

## Underlying Mechanics

Angular physics engine simulating friction, gravity, tilt angle, and ball velocity.
