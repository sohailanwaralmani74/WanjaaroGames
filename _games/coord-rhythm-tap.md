## Overview

Synchronize your taps with a steady 120 BPM audio/visual beat and measure temporal precision.

## Instructions

A metronome beats at 120 BPM. Tap along in rhythm with the beat. Your score is based on how many milliseconds off-beat each tap lands (aim for ±15ms accuracy!).

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Tap with a relaxed wrist and internalize the pulse in your body rather than watching the visual flash.

## Underlying Mechanics

AudioContext timestamp phase comparison with millisecond offset delta scoring.
