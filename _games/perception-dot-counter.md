## Overview

A scatter of dots appears for half a second. Estimate the exact count.

## Instructions

Dots flash on the screen for exactly 500 milliseconds and then vanish. Select the exact count from the multiple-choice options. Tests pure visual subitizing.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Divide the dots into small mental clusters of 2 or 3 rather than trying to count 1 by 1.

## Underlying Mechanics

Brief exposure cluster generator with subitizing range (3-12 dots).
