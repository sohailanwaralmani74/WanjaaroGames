## Overview

Guide your orb through a storm of incoming kinetic darts for as long as possible.

## Instructions

Move your mouse cursor or drag your finger to pilot the golden spark. Avoid all converging blue energy particles. Survive as long as you can while velocity escalates.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Make micro-movements near the center rather than darting into corners where you can get trapped.

## Underlying Mechanics

Kinetic vector trajectory simulation with circular bounding collision detection.
