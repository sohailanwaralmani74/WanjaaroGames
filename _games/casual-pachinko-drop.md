## Overview

Drop steel balls into a field of brass pins to bounce into high-multiplier prize buckets.

## Instructions

Choose your drop position at the top. Release balls into the peg field. Watch them bounce dynamically off pins. Aim for the center 10x jackpot bucket at the base.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Notice how pegs create a normal Gaussian distribution: balls naturally cluster toward the center.

## Underlying Mechanics

Galton board peg distribution physics with elastic restitution coefficients.
