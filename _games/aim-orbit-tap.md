## Overview

Tap precisely when the rotating orbital beacon enters the stationary alignment gate.

## Instructions

A satellite revolves around a circular orbital track. Tap when the satellite is exactly inside the target ring. Each round narrows the alignment window and increases orbital speed.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Track the rhythm of revolutions. Count the cadence mentally to time your tap effortlessly.

## Underlying Mechanics

Angular velocity alignment check with variable rotational frequency.
