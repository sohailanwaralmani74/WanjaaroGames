## Overview

Drop coins onto a moving motorized shelf to push piles of coins over the edge for jackpots.

## Instructions

Time your coin drops carefully so your coins land flush behind the advancing pusher shelf. Push coins off the edge into the payout tray. Collect bonus jewel tokens for multipliers.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Drop coins when the shelf is at the furthest backward point of its stroke to maximize forward displacement.

## Underlying Mechanics

Friction and shelf displacement physics simulating classic arcade coin pusher.
