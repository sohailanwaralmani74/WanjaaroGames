## Overview

Type a valid word that begins with the exact ending letter of the previous word.

## Instructions

Given a starting word (e.g., "TIGER"), type a real English word starting with "R" (e.g., "RABBIT"), then "T", and so forth. Build the longest uninterrupted chain.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Try to choose words ending in common vowels (E, A) to make your next word selection instantaneous.

## Underlying Mechanics

Dictionary validation check ensuring word chaining rules and length > 3.
