## Overview

Trace smoothly along an Archimedean spiral from outside to center at constant speed.

## Instructions

Click and drag your cursor or finger along the spiral guide line. Stay within the track boundaries. Speed and precision are scored together.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Maintain a steady, continuous glide rather than stopping and starting in jagged segments.

## Underlying Mechanics

Polar coordinates r = a + b*theta distance offset calculation.
