## Overview

Sort incoming geometric shapes into the Red, Blue, or Yellow bins at blistering speed.

## Instructions

Objects fall rapidly. Press Left Arrow / Left Button for Blue, Down for Red, Right for Yellow. Wrong sorts lose lives. Survive 60 seconds of accelerating sorting.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Classify solely by color hue; ignore the shapes of the items completely.

## Underlying Mechanics

3-way classification categorical sorting engine.
