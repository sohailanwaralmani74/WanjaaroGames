## Overview

Pilot a sleek starship through an oncoming asteroid field, collecting fuel pods to survive.

## Instructions

Use touch or arrow keys to steer your ship. Weave through incoming space debris. Collect blue fuel crystals to keep your shields powered up. Survive as long as you can.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Do not stay parked in the corners; keep to open space where you have room to dodge in all directions.

## Underlying Mechanics

Kinetic 2D space flight with inertial damping and procedural hazard spawning.
