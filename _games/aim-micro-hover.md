## Overview

Guide an electric ring along a winding metallic wire without touching the edges.

## Instructions

Click and drag the cursor ring along the winding path from Start to Goal. If your cursor drifts off the path or touches the boundary wire, the circuit buzzes and resets!

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Lower your mouse DPI if possible, and guide from the forearm rather than flexing fingers.

## Underlying Mechanics

Continuous path containment validator with collision boundary detection.
