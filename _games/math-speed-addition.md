## Overview

Solve rapid arithmetic problems against a 45-second timer to test calculation velocity.

## Instructions

Addition, subtraction, and multiplication problems appear. Type the numerical answer or select the correct card. Answer as many correct equations as possible before time runs out.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Round up to nearest tens to calculate, then adjust (e.g. 38 + 27 = 40 + 25 = 65).

## Underlying Mechanics

Algorithmic arithmetic problem generator with tiered difficulty scaling.
