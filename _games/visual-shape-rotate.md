## Overview

Determine whether two rotated 3D geometric shapes are identical or mirror reflections.

## Instructions

Two isometric shapes are shown at different angular orientations. Decide if they are the exact same shape merely rotated in 3D space, or if they are inverted chiral mirror images.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Mentally grab one corner of the shape and rotate it step-by-step around one axis at a time.

## Underlying Mechanics

Shepard & Metzler mental rotation 3D projection model testing spatial cognition.
