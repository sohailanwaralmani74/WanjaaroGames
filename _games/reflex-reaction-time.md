## Overview

Wait for the red screen to flash green, then tap immediately to measure response latency.

## Instructions

Tap or click to arm the sensor. Wait patiently while the screen is red. As soon as it flashes green, tap as fast as humanly possible. Tapping too early incurs a false-start penalty.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Relax your hand muscles. Tension slows your synaptic response time. Focus your gaze on the center of the display.
