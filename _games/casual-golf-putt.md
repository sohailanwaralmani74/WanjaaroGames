## Overview

Adjust angle and stroke power to navigate hills and sink the golf ball into the cup.

## Instructions

Drag backward from the golf ball to aim and set stroke power. Release to putt the ball across the green, around sand traps and obstacles, directly into the flag cup in least strokes.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

On curved greens, aim slightly above the cup to allow natural slope drift to carry the ball in.

## Underlying Mechanics

Kinetic ball physics with surface friction, slope vectors, and cup capture geometry.
