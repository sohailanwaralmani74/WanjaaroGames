## Overview

Rotate pipe tiles on a 4x4 grid to connect the water source directly to the drain.

## Instructions

Tap any pipe segment to rotate it 90 degrees clockwise. Align all conduits so water can flow in an unbroken path from the green inlet valve to the gold outlet reservoir.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Start tracing from both ends simultaneously: align the pipe touching the start and the pipe touching the exit.

## Underlying Mechanics

Graph connectivity search with rotational orientation nodes.
