## Overview

Push cargo crates onto the marked warehouse goal squares without trapping them in corners.

## Instructions

Use arrow keys or directional buttons to walk the warehouse worker. Push crates forward onto the golden target pads. Remember: you can only push crates, never pull them!

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Never push a crate flush into a dead corner unless that corner itself is a designated goal pad.

## Underlying Mechanics

Sokoban grid cellular automaton with undo history and goal state detection.
