## Overview

Control two separate orbs simultaneously, keeping Left in lane while steering Right past obstacles.

## Instructions

Left hand uses A/D or Left-touch; Right hand uses Arrow keys or Right-touch. Steer both orbs simultaneously to avoid hazards in twin parallel courses.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Do not alternate your gaze back and forth; look at the dividing center line to take in both lanes in peripheral vision.

## Underlying Mechanics

Split-screen multi-lane collision loop with independent input bindings.
