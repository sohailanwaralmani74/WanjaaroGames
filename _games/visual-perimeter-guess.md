## Overview

Two complex closed shapes are shown side-by-side. Choose which one has the longer perimeter.

## Instructions

Examine both geometric shapes. Despite their different shapes and concavities, decide which shape has the longer outer boundary wire. Score points for correct geometric judgements.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Shapes with deep indentations and crenellations always have significantly longer perimeters than convex shapes.
