## Overview

Match numerical fractions (e.g. 3/8, 2/3) to their corresponding visual pie chart representations.

## Instructions

A fractional value appears at the top. Four circular pie charts with shaded slices are shown. Tap the pie chart that precisely represents the target fraction.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Simplify fractions to standard benchmarks like 1/2 (50%) and 1/4 (25%) to eliminate incorrect pies.

## Underlying Mechanics

Fraction simplification and geometric area slice matching.
