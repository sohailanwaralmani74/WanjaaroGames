## Overview

Compete against an adaptive machine-learning bot that learns and counters your throwing habits.

## Instructions

Choose Rock, Paper, or Scissors. The bot tracks your Markov transition probabilities to predict what you will throw next. Reach 10 wins before the AI does!

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Humans unconsciously repeat what just won and switch away from what just lost. Counter-predict the AI!

## Underlying Mechanics

First-order Markov chain frequency transition model anticipating player tendencies.
