## Overview

Arrange geometric puzzle pieces (triangles, squares) to completely fill the target silhouette.

## Instructions

Drag and position geometric polygon pieces into the outlined target silhouette. All pieces must fit without overlapping or protruding past the boundary lines.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Place the largest triangular pieces first, then use small squares and triangles to plug remaining gaps.

## Underlying Mechanics

2D polygon union and raster pixel containment coverage evaluation.
