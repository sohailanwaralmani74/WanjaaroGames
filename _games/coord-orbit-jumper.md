## Overview

Jump your revolving dot between concentric spinning rings without colliding with spikes.

## Instructions

Your dot spins around a ring. Tap to jump outward to the next larger orbit. Avoid red hazard spikes rotating along each ring. Reach the outer stratosphere!

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Observe that outer rings rotate at different speeds. Wait for an open gap before leaping.

## Underlying Mechanics

Concentric angular velocity coordinates with radial projectile hop.
