## Overview

Deduce the mathematical pattern in a sequence of 5 numbers and select the next number.

## Instructions

Sequences like "3, 6, 12, 24, [ ? ]" or "2, 5, 10, 17, [ ? ]" are displayed. Figure out the underlying rule (addition, squaring, geometric progression) and choose the next term.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Calculate the differences between consecutive terms first. If differences increase steadily, it is quadratic.

## Underlying Mechanics

Polynomial and geometric sequence generator with distractor options.
