## Overview

Adjust the slider until the target center circle matches the reference circle diameter.

## Instructions

Optical illusions make circles surrounded by large rings appear smaller, and vice-versa. Drag the slider until both center circles are identical in true diameter, then press Submit.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Compare the edge-to-edge distance across the circle against an external mental ruler.
