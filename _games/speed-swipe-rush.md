## Overview

Instantly press the arrow key or swipe matching the on-screen arrow prompt.

## Instructions

Arrows (Up, Down, Left, Right) flash in rapid succession. Hit the matching arrow key or swipe the corresponding direction before the prompt timer expires.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Rest four fingers on arrow keys (or WASD) so each direction has a dedicated finger ready.

## Underlying Mechanics

Rapid directional stimulus-response loop with acceleration pacing.
