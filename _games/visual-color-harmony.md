## Overview

Arrange shuffled color tiles in sequential order to form a smooth chromatic rainbow gradient.

## Instructions

Tiles with subtle hue differences are shuffled out of order. Drag and drop tiles into the correct slots so color flows seamlessly from red, orange, yellow, green, to cyan.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Place the extreme anchor colors on the outer edges first, then fill in mid-tones.

## Underlying Mechanics

Farnsworth-Munsell 100 Hue color order distance calculation.
