## Overview

Keep the bouncing pong ball alive against a resilient wall to build the longest rally streak.

## Instructions

Control your paddle vertically or horizontally. Keep the ball bouncing against the opposite bounce wall without letting it slip past your paddle. Speed escalates after every 5 rallies.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Move your paddle into position before the ball crosses mid-court rather than reacting at the last second.

## Underlying Mechanics

Classic paddle physics with speed multiplier per bounce and spin physics.
