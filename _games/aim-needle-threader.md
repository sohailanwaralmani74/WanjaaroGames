## Overview

Guide an oscillating thread through rapidly moving needle eyes without snagging.

## Instructions

Hold spacebar or touch screen to raise the thread line; release to lower it. Thread the line cleanly through the eyes of the moving needles as they scroll by.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Feather your taps in micro-bursts to maintain a stable horizontal altitude.

## Underlying Mechanics

Continuous oscillation wave modulation with narrow aperture clearance detection.
