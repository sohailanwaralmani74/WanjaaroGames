## Overview

Two abstract geometric symbols flash on screen. Rapidly decide if they are identical or different.

## Instructions

Two symbols appear side by side. Hit "SAME" if they match exactly in shape and rotation; hit "DIFFERENT" if any feature differs. Score as many correct answers in 30 seconds.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Check one distinctive component (like orientation or inner cutouts) first to make instantaneous decisions.

## Underlying Mechanics

Visual comparison verification with latency and accuracy multiplier.
