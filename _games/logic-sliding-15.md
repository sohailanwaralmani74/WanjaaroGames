## Overview

Slide tiles 1 through 8 in a 3x3 grid into proper ascending order in minimum moves.

## Instructions

Click or tap any tile adjacent to the empty slot to slide it into the empty space. Arrange tiles in order from 1 to 8 with the empty slot at the bottom-right.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Solve the top row (1, 2, 3) first, then solve the remaining rows from left to right.

## Underlying Mechanics

Classic 8-puzzle sliding tile inversion-parity verified scrambler.
