## Overview

Deflect the bouncing chrome sphere using your horizontal paddle to demolish all bricks.

## Instructions

Slide your paddle left and right. Bounce the ball into the brick wall. Angle your paddle strikes to send the ball into hard-to-reach pockets. Catch power-ups for laser and wide paddle!

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Hitting the ball with the outer edge of your paddle launches it at a shallow, high-speed angle.

## Underlying Mechanics

2D continuous collision detection with paddle strike offset angle reflection.
