## Overview

Memorize the locations of highlighted tiles on a grid, then tap them once hidden.

## Instructions

A set of tiles will glow for 1.5 seconds. Once they turn blank, tap the exact tiles that were highlighted. The grid size and count increase as your level rises.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Look at the overall geometric silhouette created by the illuminated tiles rather than individual squares.

## Underlying Mechanics

Spatial working memory test (Corsi Block Tapping variant) with progressive grid scales.
