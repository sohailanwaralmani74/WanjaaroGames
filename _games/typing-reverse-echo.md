## Overview

Type the displayed words in reverse order (e.g., "SWIFT" -> "TFIWS").

## Instructions

A word appears on screen. Type its letters in reverse order from right to left as fast as possible. Tests your brain's ability to invert mental phonemes.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Focus your eyes on the last letter of the word first and read backward.

## Underlying Mechanics

String reversal verification engine with per-word latency scoring.
