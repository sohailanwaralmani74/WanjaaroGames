## Overview

Stop the charging speed gauge as close to 100% as possible without overflowing into the red.

## Instructions

Press or tap to start the gauge needle accelerating. Tap Stop before it exceeds the critical red threshold line. The closer to 100% without going over, the higher your score.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Watch the acceleration curve: the gauge speeds up exponentially near the 80% mark.

## Underlying Mechanics

Variable acceleration curve with threshold overrun penalization.
