## Overview

Pop bubbles floating up the screen; larger bubbles yield 1 pt, tiny swift bubbles yield 3 pts.

## Instructions

Click or tap bubbles rising from bottom to top. Pop as many as possible within 30 seconds. Golden bubbles trigger 2x double point frenzy!

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Focus on popping clusters at the bottom before they spread out across the top.

## Underlying Mechanics

Particle bubble physics with radius-weighted point allocation.
