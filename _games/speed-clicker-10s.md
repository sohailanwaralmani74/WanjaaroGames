## Overview

Click or tap the button as many times as possible within a strict 10-second countdown.

## Instructions

Tap the target button as fast as you can. A 10-second timer begins on your first click. Your final score is your total clicks and Clicks Per Second (CPS) rate.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Jitter-clicking (tensing your forearm to vibrate your finger) or butterfly-clicking can boost CPS dramatically.

## Underlying Mechanics

High-frequency pointer event accumulator with millisecond countdown timer.
