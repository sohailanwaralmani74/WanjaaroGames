## Overview

Draw lines between grid dots. Whoever draws the 4th side of a 1x1 box claims it and takes another turn.

## Instructions

Take turns drawing horizontal or vertical lines between adjacent dots. Completing a 4-sided box captures it for 1 point and gives you an immediate extra turn. Capture more boxes than the bot.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Avoid creating "long chains" of 3-sided boxes early on that hand the entire chain to your opponent.

## Underlying Mechanics

Graph edge assignment engine with closed loop cycle detection.
