## Overview

Steer the classic serpent, consume nutritious energy pellets, and grow without crashing.

## Instructions

Use arrow keys or swipe controls to guide the snake. Eat the glowing food pellets to increase length and score. Crashing into walls or your own tail terminates the game.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Circumnavigate the outer walls in a winding serpentine loop to avoid trapping your tail.

## Underlying Mechanics

Tile-based array deque queue position updates with self-intersection collision.
