## Overview

Play against an unbeatable Minimax AI; test if you can hold it to a draw or catch a flaw.

## Instructions

Place your X on the 3x3 grid. Form three-in-a-row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Choose between Medium AI or Master Minimax bot that calculates all game trees.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Always claim the center square or an opposite corner on your first moves to avoid getting forked.

## Underlying Mechanics

Minimax decision tree evaluator with alpha-beta pruning.
