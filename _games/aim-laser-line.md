## Overview

Rotate optical mirrors so the reflective laser beam passes through all target crystals.

## Instructions

Drag or turn the rotating mirrors on the board. Direct the reflective laser beam from the transmitter through every glowing crystal simultaneously.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Work backwards from the target crystals to identify which mirror needs to reflect the terminal ray.

## Underlying Mechanics

Angle of incidence = angle of reflection raycasting solver.
