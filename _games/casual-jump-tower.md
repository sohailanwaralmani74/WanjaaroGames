## Overview

Bounce continuously upward across moving floating platforms to reach supreme heights.

## Instructions

Your character bounces automatically upon landing. Steer left or right to land on platforms as the camera scrolls upward. Steer past moving springs for super jumps.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

You can wrap around the screen edges on most platforms: exiting the left edge re-enters on the right.

## Underlying Mechanics

Vertical platformer physics with camera viewport tracking and jump velocity.
