## Overview

Select 2 or 3 numbers from a 3x3 grid that add up exactly to the target number.

## Instructions

A target sum (e.g. 27) is shown alongside a grid of single and double-digit integers. Select the combination of numbers that sums exactly to the target. Multiple solutions may exist!

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Start with the largest number smaller than the target, then look for the exact remainder.

## Underlying Mechanics

Subset sum problem validator with integer search space.
