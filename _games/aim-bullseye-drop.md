## Overview

Release a dropping metal sphere so gravity carries it directly into a moving basket.

## Instructions

A basket oscillates horizontally at the bottom. Tap the Release button at the exact moment so the falling sphere lands directly in the basket center.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Account for gravitational acceleration: the ball travels much faster through the bottom half of its descent.

## Underlying Mechanics

Newtonian projectile drop with constant gravity acceleration (g = 9.8m/s² equivalent).
