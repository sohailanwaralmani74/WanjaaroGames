## Overview

Launch an orb around celestial gravity wells to enter the wormhole portal.

## Instructions

Pull back on the launch orb to set trajectory angle and power. Release to sling past gravitational bodies and land in the portal ring. Complete in fewest launches.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Use planetary gravity to whip the probe around obstacles rather than aiming in a straight line.

## Underlying Mechanics

N-body gravitational attraction field calculation with trajectory preview.
