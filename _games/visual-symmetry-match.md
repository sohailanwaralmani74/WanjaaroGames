## Overview

The left half of a pixel grid is painted. Tap the right half to create a perfect mirror symmetry.

## Instructions

A vertical mirror line divides the canvas. Replicate the illuminated tiles on the right side so the image displays bilateral symmetry. Complete in minimum seconds.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Measure tile distance outward from the center dividing line rather than counting from the right edge.

## Underlying Mechanics

Bilateral reflective coordinate transformation check across x-axis center.
