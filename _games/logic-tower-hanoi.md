## Overview

Move all disks from Peg A to Peg C without ever placing a larger disk onto a smaller one.

## Instructions

Click a peg to pick up its top disk, then click a destination peg. You may only move one disk at a time, and no disk may ever sit atop a smaller disk. Complete in minimum 7 moves for 3 disks.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

For an odd number of disks, your very first move must always place the smallest disk onto the target peg.

## Underlying Mechanics

Recursive mathematical puzzle solver with move verification and minimum-move tracking.
