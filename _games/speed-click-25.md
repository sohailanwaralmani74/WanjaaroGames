## Overview

Tap numbers 1 through 25 in ascending sequential order as fast as humanly possible.

## Instructions

A 5x5 grid containing numbers 1 to 25 scrambled randomly. Click number 1, then 2, then 3, all the way to 25. Used by cosmonauts and speed-readers to train peripheral vision.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Keep your eyes anchored in the exact center of the 5x5 board and use peripheral vision to locate numbers.

## Underlying Mechanics

Ascending sequence validator with 1-millisecond timer resolution.
