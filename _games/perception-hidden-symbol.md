## Overview

Locate the hidden unique glyph disguised amongst visual pattern noise.

## Instructions

Search through a dense field of repeating geometric distractors to find the single unique symbol. Tap it as fast as possible to progress to harder stages.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Scan systematically in horizontal rows or quadrants rather than jumping your eyes randomly.

## Underlying Mechanics

Visual feature conjunction search against dense distractor fields.
