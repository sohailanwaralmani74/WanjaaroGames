## Overview

Flip hidden cards to find matching pairs in as few moves and seconds as possible.

## Instructions

Click a card to reveal its face icon, then click a second card. If they match, they remain open. If not, they flip back over. Clear the entire 16-card deck.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Mentally tag each card position with a coordinate (e.g., Top-Left is Star, Row 2 Column 3 is Heart).

## Underlying Mechanics

Classic concentration matching game tracking flip count and duration.
