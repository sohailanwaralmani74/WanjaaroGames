## Overview

Press all 26 letters of the English alphabet from A to Z in sequential order as fast as possible.

## Instructions

Press Start, then type A, B, C... through Z without making a single mistake. The clock stops the instant you hit Z. Elite typists achieve sub-2.0 seconds!

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Position your hands over both halves of the keyboard and visualize the physical path between each letter.

## Underlying Mechanics

Sequential alphabetical keystroke latency recorder with 1-millisecond resolution.
