## Overview

Quickly decide if the displayed integer is PRIME or COMPOSITE before the clock ticks down.

## Instructions

A number between 2 and 150 appears. Hit PRIME if it has no divisors other than 1 and itself; hit COMPOSITE if it has factors. Win points for speed and accuracy.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Check divisibility by 2 (even), 3 (sum of digits divisible by 3), and 5 (ends in 0 or 5).

## Underlying Mechanics

Trial division primality test with real-time response timer.
