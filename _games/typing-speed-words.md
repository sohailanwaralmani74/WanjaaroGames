## Overview

Type words accurately against a 60-second timer to measure your Net Words Per Minute.

## Instructions

Type the highlighted word and press Spacebar to proceed to the next word. Inaccurate keystrokes highlight red. The test concludes after 60 seconds with full WPM and accuracy metrics.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Prioritize accuracy over frantic speed. Fixing typing errors takes three times longer than typing calmly.

## Underlying Mechanics

High-precision WPM calculation engine with character error accounting.
