## Overview

Plot the exact reflected coordinates of a point across a 45-degree diagonal mirror line.

## Instructions

A point is marked on a coordinate grid alongside a diagonal mirror line (y = x). Tap the exact grid coordinate where the point will appear after reflection across the mirror.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Reflecting across y = x simply swaps the X and Y coordinates: point (3, 7) reflects to (7, 3).

## Underlying Mechanics

Linear algebra coordinate transformation matrix [[0, 1], [1, 0]] validation.
