## Overview

Memorize the ordering of 5 colorful gradient swatches, then recreate the palette.

## Instructions

A palette of five hues appears for 3 seconds. After they are shuffled, click each color in its original left-to-right order to restore the palette.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Name the colors silently in your head as a rhythmic sentence (e.g., "Teal, Gold, Ruby, Violet, Cyan").

## Underlying Mechanics

Chromatic order serial memory evaluation.
