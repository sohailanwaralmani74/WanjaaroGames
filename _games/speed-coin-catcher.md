## Overview

Move your basket to catch falling gold coins while dodging falling black anvils.

## Instructions

Slide basket left and right with mouse or touch. Catch shimmering gold coins for +10 pts. Touching a falling anvil deducts a heart! Collect maximum coins in 45 seconds.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Position your basket underneath dense coin clusters rather than chasing lone coins on far edges.

## Underlying Mechanics

Kinetic 1D paddle collision with coin and hazard entity arrays.
