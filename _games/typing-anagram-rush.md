## Overview

Unscramble jumbled 5-letter words before the fuse burns down.

## Instructions

A scrambled anagram appears (e.g. "T R E A H" -> "EARTH"). Type the correct unscrambled English word and press Enter. Solve as many anagrams as you can in 60 seconds.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Look for common vowel pairings (EA, OU, AI) and consonant blends (TH, ST, CH) to spot the root word.

## Underlying Mechanics

Dictionary validation against curated English lexicon.
