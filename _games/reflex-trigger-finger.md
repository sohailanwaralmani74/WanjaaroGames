## Overview

Targets pop up for brief fractions of a second. Tap them before they vanish.

## Instructions

Target discs flash on screen for only 400 milliseconds. Tap or click them before they disappear. Missing 3 targets terminates your run.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Rest your mouse wrist or holding fingers lightly to reduce muscular drag.

## Underlying Mechanics

Brief exposure target detection requiring instantaneous motor response.
