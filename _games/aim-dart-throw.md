## Overview

Flick or drag darts towards the classic dartboard to score triple-20s and bullseyes.

## Instructions

Drag back and flick upward towards the board. The flick vector controls velocity and trajectory angle. Aim for the inner red ring (Triple 20) and center Bullseye.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

A smooth, straight vertical drag produces far more consistent accuracy than an erratic quick swipe.

## Underlying Mechanics

Swipe vector momentum transfer with simulated 2.5D ballistic trajectory.
