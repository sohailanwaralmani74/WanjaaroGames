## Overview

Find the single tile with a subtly different shade of color among the grid.

## Instructions

A grid of colored squares is presented. One square is slightly different in hue or saturation. Click the outlier square. Each round increases grid density and narrows the color delta.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Slightly defocus your eyes or look at the whole grid at once. The outlier tile will subtly "pop" out.

## Underlying Mechanics

HSL color space delta reduction with adaptive grid scaling (2x2 up to 8x8).
