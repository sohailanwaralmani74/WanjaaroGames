## Overview

Auditory reaction test: Close your eyes and click the instant the high-pitch chime rings.

## Instructions

Click Start to enter listening mode. Keep your finger poised over the button or spacebar. When the chime sounds, click immediately. Your score is your pure acoustic reaction time.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Human auditory reactions are typically 20-40ms faster than visual reactions because auditory signals reach the brainstem quicker.

## Underlying Mechanics

Auditory cortex latency test using Web Audio API synthesis without visual cues.
