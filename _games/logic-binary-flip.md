## Overview

Toggle 8 bits (128, 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1) to match the target decimal number.

## Instructions

A target decimal number between 0 and 255 is displayed. Click the binary bit toggles (0 or 1) to make the sum of active power-of-two bits equal the target as fast as possible.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Find the largest power of two that fits into the number, flip it on, subtract it, and repeat for the remainder.

## Underlying Mechanics

Base-2 positional numeral system interactive converter.
