## Overview

Type falling letter raindrops before they touch the ground to maintain clean water level.

## Instructions

Letters drop from the top of the canvas. Press the corresponding key on your keyboard or on-screen key to vaporize the letter before it hits the bottom.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Keep your fingers resting on the home row (ASDF JKL;) so you can reach any letter with minimal finger travel.

## Underlying Mechanics

Dynamic falling entity spawner with keyboard event interception.
