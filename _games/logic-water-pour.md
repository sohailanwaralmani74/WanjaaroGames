## Overview

Use 3-liter and 5-liter jugs to measure out exactly 4 liters of water into the basin.

## Instructions

You have a 3L jug, a 5L jug, and an endless water tap. Fill, empty, and pour water between jugs until you have measured the exact target quantity. Classic Die Hard riddle!

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Fill the 5L jug first, pour into the 3L jug (leaving 2L in 5L), empty the 3L jug, and pour the 2L over.

## Underlying Mechanics

Diophantine equation water jug state machine with valid capacity checks.
