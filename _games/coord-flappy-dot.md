## Overview

Tap to pulse upward and guide your aero dot cleanly through vertical pipe gaps.

## Instructions

Press Space or tap screen to give your dot an upward aerodynamic impulse. Let gravity pull you downward. Thread smoothly through gaps in scrolling obstacles.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Tap rhythmically in small bursts right before entering a gap to establish a flat glide.

## Underlying Mechanics

Delta-time gravity and impulse velocity physics with rectangular pipe AABB collision.
