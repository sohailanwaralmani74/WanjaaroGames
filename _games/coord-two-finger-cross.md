## Overview

Simultaneously drag two dots through independently opening gates on both sides of the screen.

## Instructions

Place one finger / mouse on the Left Dot and one on the Right Dot. Guide both through their respective security gates when both gates open simultaneously.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Wait for both gates to cycle open before launching both hands forward together.

## Underlying Mechanics

Dual touch / multi-pointer trackable coordinate event handler.
