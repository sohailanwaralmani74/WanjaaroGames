## Overview

Keep the chrome pinball in play using Left and Right flippers to trigger bumpers and ramps.

## Instructions

Press Left Arrow / A for left flipper and Right Arrow / D for right flipper (or tap screen sides). Knock the silver ball into bonus bumpers and loops to rack up high scores.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Cradle the ball by holding the flipper up when it rolls down slowly, allowing you to aim your next shot.

## Underlying Mechanics

Rigid body angular flipper impulses and circular bumper bounce restitution.
