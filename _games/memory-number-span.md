## Overview

A sequence of numbers flashes on screen one by one. Enter them from memory.

## Instructions

Numbers appear sequentially on screen. Once the sequence ends, type or tap the numbers in the identical sequence. Reaching 8+ digits places you in the top 5% of recall.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Group digits into rhythmic chunks of three or four, like phone numbers (e.g. 5-8-2 then 9-1-4).

## Underlying Mechanics

Standardized psychological digit span memory capacity test.
