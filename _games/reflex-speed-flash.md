## Overview

Tap when the white beacon appears, but avoid tapping when the red decoy flashes.

## Instructions

Beacons will appear intermittently. Tap instantly when the beacon is WHITE. If the beacon is RED, hold your fire! Scores are calculated from reaction time minus false-tap penalties.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Do not click on anticipation. Wait for the color signal to register in your visual cortex before engaging muscles.

## Underlying Mechanics

Go/No-Go stimulus response paradigm testing selective attention under strict time constraints.
