## Overview

Tap when the target color matches the active background field.

## Instructions

A color name and card cycle rapidly. When the color card matches the primary banner color, tap immediately! Match streaks grant score multipliers.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Pay attention to the overarching saturation shift rather than examining fine borders.

## Underlying Mechanics

Continuous visual matching with rapid temporal cycling and streak multipliers.
