## Overview

Find and trace the path through a randomly generated maze from entrance to exit.

## Instructions

Use arrow keys or touch swipe to navigate the green spark through the labyrinth to the golden goal flag. Watch out for dead ends. Solved mazes increase in complexity.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Trace the path backward from the exit to the entrance to spot the true corridor faster.

## Underlying Mechanics

Wilson's / Prim's algorithm perfect maze generation with A* path validation.
