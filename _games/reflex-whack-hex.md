## Overview

Active hexagons light up across the matrix. Tap them before their illumination fades.

## Instructions

A cluster of honeycomb cells will randomly ignite. Click or tap each illuminated cell before its countdown expires. The game accelerates every 5 successful hits.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Keep your gaze focused on the center of the honeycomb to exploit peripheral vision rather than scanning cell by cell.

## Underlying Mechanics

Multi-target visual search and spatial motor execution with decaying timeout windows.
