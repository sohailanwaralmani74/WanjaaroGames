## Overview

Watch and listen to the expanding sequence of colored chimes, then repeat it back.

## Instructions

The platform plays a sequence of illuminated tones. Repeat the sequence in the exact same order. Each successful round adds one more step to the chain.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Chunk sequences into triplets or associate numbers (1-4) with each color tone.

## Underlying Mechanics

Incremental sequential working memory loop with audio-visual reinforcement.
