## Overview

Fill in the missing arithmetic operator (+, -, ×, ÷) that makes the equality mathematically true.

## Instructions

An equation appears with a missing symbol: 18 [ ? ] 6 = 3. Click the correct operator (+, -, ×, or ÷). Complete 15 equations in the fewest seconds.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Check if the result is dramatically smaller than the operands (division) or larger (multiplication).
