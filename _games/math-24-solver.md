## Overview

Use four given numbers and operators (+, -, *, /) to create an equation that equals exactly 24.

## Instructions

Four numbers are drawn (e.g. 3, 3, 8, 8). Combine them using basic arithmetic operations so the final result is 24 (e.g. 8 / (3 - 8/3) = 24). Tap numbers and signs in sequence.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Look for common factors of 24: 3 x 8, 4 x 6, 2 x 12, or 25 - 1.

## Underlying Mechanics

Combinatorial expression tree evaluator verifying equality to 24.
