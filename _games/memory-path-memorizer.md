## Overview

A glowing path slithers through a dark maze. Trace the exact identical path.

## Instructions

Observe the bright trail moving from start to finish. When prompted, swipe or click adjacent cells to reproduce the exact route taken without making wrong turns.

## Strategy & Pro Tips

Mentally encode the turns as directional prompts (e.g., "Right 2, Up 3, Right 1, Down 2").

## Underlying Mechanics

Directional vector path recording and verification on orthogonal grid.
