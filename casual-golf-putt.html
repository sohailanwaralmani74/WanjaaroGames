🎯 Objective

Modulate stroke angle and power to putt the golf ball across turf slopes and into the cup in the fewest strokes.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Drag backward from the golf ball to aim and set stroke power. Release to putt the ball across the green, around sand traps and obstacles, directly into the flag cup in least strokes.

Scored in moves (lower values indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This sports physics game measures kinetic force modulation and surface slope trajectory estimation. It tests your judgment of ball friction, bank angles, and cup capture speed.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Pull back gently on short putts; hitting the cup with excessive speed will cause the ball to lip out.
  • 2. Read green contour arrows: aim uphill of the hole to allow natural slope break to steer the ball toward the cup.
  • 3. Use wooden boundary walls to bank shots around sand traps and water hazards.
  • 4. Avoid sand bunkers, as landing in sand dramatically deadens ball roll and adds penalty strokes.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do you aim and set stroke power in Mini Golf Hole-in-One?

Drag backward from the golf ball to draw an aiming arrow; pulling farther back increases stroke velocity.

What is par for each hole in Mini Golf?

Standard holes have a par rating between 2 and 4 strokes, with hole-in-one aces awarding massive bonus points.

Can you putt backwards if you get stuck behind an obstacle?

Yes, you can aim 360 degrees in any direction to bounce off boundary walls and escape tricky corners.
