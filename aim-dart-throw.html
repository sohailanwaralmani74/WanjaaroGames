🎯 Objective

Flick your cursor with precise velocity and release angle to plant darts into the dartboard bullseye.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Drag back and flick upward towards the board. The flick vector controls velocity and trajectory angle. Aim for the inner red ring (Triple 20) and center Bullseye.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This game measures gesture flick release speed and angular directional consistency. It benchmarks kinetic gesture control in simulated projectile mechanics.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Swipe straight forward through the center line of the dartboard for optimal directional stability.
  • 2. Vary your swipe speed rather than distance to control dart trajectory depth.
  • 3. Release smoothly at the apex of your upward swipe to avoid downward pull.
  • 4. Practice landing three consecutive darts in the triple-20 ring to build muscle memory.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is throwing power measured from the mouse swipe?

The release velocity is calculated from the pixel distance covered in the final 50 milliseconds of your flick gesture.

Can crosswinds affect the dart path?

Advanced stages introduce gentle horizontal wind gusts indicated by a directional arrow at the top of the board.

Are standard 501 dart rules supported?

The primary game mode is an arcade high-score target shootout, with an optional 301 countdown mode available.
