🎯 Objective

Drag vertex nodes across the canvas until no two connecting line segments cross or intersect each other.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

A graph of interconnected vertices begins in a tangled knot with crossing red lines. Drag the nodes until every line turns blue and no two lines intersect. A pure topology puzzle.

Scored in moves (lower values indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This puzzle measures planar graph untangling and topology visualization. It evaluates your spatial reorganization of intersecting nodes into non-overlapping planar embeddings.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Move nodes with the highest number of connecting edges toward the outside perimeter of the circle.
  • 2. Place nodes with few connections near the center where they are less likely to cross paths.
  • 3. Look for triangles or loops of three nodes and spread their corners wide apart.
  • 4. Work on resolving one intersecting line crossover at a time rather than shifting everything at once.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is every graph in Planar Graph Untangler guaranteed to be solvable?

Yes, all generated graphs are mathematically planar, meaning they can always be untangled with zero line crossings.

How do you know when a line is untangled?

Intersecting lines glow bright red, while fully cleared non-intersecting lines turn solid emerald green.

What is the Kuratowski theorem related to planar graphs?

Kuratowski's theorem states that a finite graph is planar if and only if it does not contain subdivisions of K5 or K3,3 utility graphs.
