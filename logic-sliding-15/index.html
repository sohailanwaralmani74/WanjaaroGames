🎯 Objective

Slide numbered tiles one space at a time into the empty slot to arrange numbers 1 through 8 or 15 in numerical order.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Click or tap any tile adjacent to the empty slot to slide it into the empty space. Arrange tiles in order from 1 to 8 with the empty slot at the bottom-right.

Scored in moves (lower values indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This puzzle measures combinatorial state manipulation and sequential spatial planning. It benchmarks your efficiency at navigating physical grid constraints to assemble an ordered layout.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Solve the top row first from left to right, locking numbers in their final positions.
  • 2. Solve the left-most column next, reducing the remaining puzzle into a smaller sub-grid.
  • 3. To insert the final two numbers in a row, line them up vertically then rotate them into place together.
  • 4. Never move completed top-row numbers once they are anchored in position.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are all sliding tile shuffles solvable?

Only permutations with an even parity of inversions are solvable; all boards generated here are verified solvable.

What is the difference between the 8-puzzle and 15-puzzle?

The 8-puzzle uses a 3x3 grid (8 tiles), while the 15-puzzle uses a 4x4 grid (15 tiles) requiring more planning steps.

Can you slide multiple tiles at once in a line?

Yes, clicking a tile in the same row or column as the open slot pushes all intermediate tiles in a single stroke.
