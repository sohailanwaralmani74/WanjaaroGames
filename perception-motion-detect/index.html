🎯 Objective

Identify the prevailing directional flow of coherent dots within a swarm of random background noise.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

In a swarm of 100 jittery dots, 15% move coherently in one cardinal direction (Up, Down, Left, or Right). Detect and click the direction of coherent drift.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This test measures coherent visual motion detection and signal-to-noise perceptual threshold. It records the minimum percentage of aligned moving dots required for you to correctly identify overall direction.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Gaze at the overall field rather than trying to track single isolated dots.
  • 2. Allow your eyes to perceive the global flow drift (up, down, left, or right) across the entire patch.
  • 3. Look through the display center; global motion signals emerge most clearly across a broad visual field.
  • 4. Take your time; dots circulate for several seconds to allow the coherent direction to resolve.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What percentage of dots move in the target direction?

Coherence begins at 50% on early levels and scales down to a challenging 10% on expert stages.

What are the four possible directions in Coherent Motion Spotter?

Dots flow along cardinal axes: Up, Down, Left, or Right, selected randomly each round.

How is motion coherence used in scientific research?

Random dot kinematograms are widely used in vision science to test how the visual system integrates fragmented motion signals.
