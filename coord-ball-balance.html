🎯 Objective

Keep a rolling metal ball balanced on a tilting platform for as long as possible without letting it fall off.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Use Left/Right keys or tap sides of the screen to tilt the seesaw platform. Counteract gravity and momentum to keep the ball from rolling off the platform edges.

Scored in sec (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This simulation measures dynamic equilibrium control and corrective counter-steering. It evaluates how you modulate tilt angles to stabilize a moving rolling mass.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Make tiny, gentle counter-tilts; aggressive movements will cause the ball to pick up uncontrollable speed.
  • 2. Keep the ball near the center crosshair to give yourself recovery margin in every direction.
  • 3. When the ball begins rolling fast, tilt gently in the opposite direction before it reaches the outer perimeter.
  • 4. Breathe steadily and avoid over-reacting to small wobbles.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does the game support device gyroscope controls?

On supported mobile devices, tilting your physical phone directly controls platform inclination via device orientation sensors.

How does ball friction affect rolling speed?

The platform simulates low-friction polished metal, meaning momentum carries the ball smoothly across tilts.

Are there obstacles or wind gusts on the platform?

Advanced survival stages introduce gentle platform wind gusts and moving surface bumper pegs.
