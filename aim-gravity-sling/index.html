💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Use the dotted trajectory forecast line to observe how planet gravity wells bend your probe path.
  • 2. Launch close to a massive planet to achieve a fast slingshot acceleration boost.
  • 3. Avoid orbits that graze planet surfaces too closely, as atmospheric drag will collapse your probe.
  • 4. Aim for the outer edge of gravitational wells to achieve gentle curving deflections.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does planet mass influence probe trajectory in Gravity Slingshot?

Larger, denser planets exert stronger gravitational pull, curving your flight path into tighter orbital arcs.

Can you adjust probe trajectory once launched?

No, launches are ballistic. All angle and thrust settings must be locked in before releasing the launch catapult.

How do you earn three stars on gravity levels?

Collect all three floating orbital energy crystals before entering the destination wormhole in a single launch.
