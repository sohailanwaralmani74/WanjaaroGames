🎯 Objective

Observe a brief palette of distinct color strips, then arrange the scrambled swatches into their original sequence.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

A palette of five hues appears for 3 seconds. After they are shuffled, click each color in its original left-to-right order to restore the palette.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This drill measures visual hue sequence retention. It tests how accurately you store chromatic ordering without verbal digit crutches.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Create a narrative mnemonic linking the colors (for example: red sun, green grass, blue ocean).
  • 2. Pay special attention to the first and last colors in the spectrum bar.
  • 3. Note warm versus cool color clusters to reduce sequence complexity.
  • 4. Double check your assembled spectrum before submitting your final arrangement.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many colors appear in the maximum sequence?

Sequences start with four swatches on stage one and expand up to eight distinct colors on master stages.

Are colors in Palette Spectrum Recall randomly generated?

Yes, swatches are chosen from calibrated high-contrast color wheels to prevent ambiguous tints.

How does dragging work on mobile screens?

Touch and slide any color swatch horizontally to reorder positions smoothly with live previews.
