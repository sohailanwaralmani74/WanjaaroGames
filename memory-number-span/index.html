🎯 Objective

Memorize an expanding string of random digits displayed one by one, then type them back accurately.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Numbers appear sequentially on screen. Once the sequence ends, type or tap the numbers in the identical sequence. Reaching 8+ digits places you in the top 5% of recall.

Scored in streak (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This test measures forward auditory-verbal working memory span. It records the maximum length of an un-grouped numerical sequence you can hold and reproduce.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Sub-vocalize digits in chunks of three (like phone numbers: 4-8-1, 9-2-5) rather than single digits.
  • 2. Focus intensely on the final two digits, which fade the fastest from immediate recall.
  • 3. Type your answer in a steady, unbroken rhythm once the prompt input field unlocks.
  • 4. Avoid looking away from the screen while digits are flashing in sequence.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard benchmark for human digit span?

In 1956, psychologist George Miller identified that human short-term memory typically holds 7 ± 2 items (5 to 9 digits).

How fast do the digits flash on screen?

Each digit appears individually for 800 milliseconds followed by a 200 millisecond blank interval.

What is the difference between forward and backward digit span?

Forward digit span tests raw capacity, while backward digit span requires active mental manipulation before typing.
