🎯 Objective

Click or tap the big target button as many times as humanly possible within a 10-second endurance sprint.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Tap the target button as fast as you can. A 10-second timer begins on your first click. Your final score is your total clicks and Clicks Per Second (CPS) rate.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This benchmark measures clicks per second (CPS) and short-term motor endurance. It tests raw muscular firing velocity and repetitive trigger stamina.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Grip the mouse lightly between your thumb and ring finger while vibrating your index finger over the switch.
  • 2. Use the jitter-click technique (tensing your forearm to create a controlled tremor) if seeking elite scores.
  • 3. Ensure your mouse has crisp optical switches with short actuation travel for minimal bounce delay.
  • 4. Pace yourself slightly to avoid running out of stamina in the final three seconds of the sprint.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is an average clicks-per-second (CPS) rating?

Everyday mouse users click between 6.0 and 7.5 CPS; trained Minecraft PvP players reach 12.0 to 16.0+ CPS.

Can you use auto-clickers or macro scripts in this drill?

The engine monitors millisecond inter-click variance and flags mathematically uniform mechanical inputs.

Does the test support double-clicking or drag-clicking mice?

Any hardware click registered by your operating system is recorded directly by the benchmark counter.
