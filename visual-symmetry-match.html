🎯 Objective

Complete the missing half of a symmetrical pixel art grid across a central vertical or horizontal mirror axis.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

A vertical mirror line divides the canvas. Replicate the illuminated tiles on the right side so the image displays bilateral symmetry. Complete in minimum seconds.

Scored in sec (lower values indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This game measures bilateral symmetry perception and coordinate mirror reflection. It tests your accuracy in translating coordinates across a reflection axis.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Count outward from the center mirror line rather than counting inward from the outer wall.
  • 2. Paint pixel blocks in symmetrical pairs: one unit left of center maps to one unit right of center.
  • 3. Look at overall silhouette outlines before filling internal decorative pixel squares.
  • 4. Use drag-to-paint to fill continuous symmetrical lines swiftly.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can the mirror axis be horizontal as well as vertical?

Yes, early stages use vertical reflection axes, while advanced levels introduce horizontal and diagonal mirror axes.

What grid sizes are used in Symmetry Mirror Painter?

Grids scale from 8x8 pixels on casual puzzles up to 16x16 pixels on complex tapestries.

How is painting accuracy scored?

Scores reflect the percentage of correctly placed mirror pixels minus deductions for misplaced blocks.
