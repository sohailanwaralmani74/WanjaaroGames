🎯 Objective

Compare two irregular geometric polygons and determine which one possesses the longer outer perimeter.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Examine both geometric shapes. Despite their different shapes and concavities, decide which shape has the longer outer boundary wire. Score points for correct geometric judgements.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This geometry drill measures contour length estimation and perimeter scaling perception. It evaluates your judgment of total boundary distance across jagged versus smooth shapes.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Remember that jagged shapes with many indentations have longer perimeters than smooth shapes of equal area.
  • 2. Mentally unfold line segments into straight lines to compare total length.
  • 3. Do not be fooled by overall surface area; a thin star can have a much larger perimeter than a fat circle.
  • 4. Count the number of sharp vertices; each indentation adds substantial perimeter distance.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between area and perimeter in this game?

Area measures the internal surface space, while perimeter measures strictly the continuous outer boundary distance.

Can two shapes have equal perimeters in Perimeter Length Comparer?

Select bonus rounds feature identical perimeters to test whether you can recognize parity despite unequal areas.

How is performance rated?

Scores reflect accuracy percentage and average judgment latency across 10 progressive shape pairs.
