🎯 Objective

Drop shiny coins onto the sliding upper deck to push accumulating heaps of coins and prize tokens off the edge.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Time your coin drops carefully so your coins land flush behind the advancing pusher shelf. Push coins off the edge into the payout tray. Collect bonus jewel tokens for multipliers.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This arcade physics simulator measures timing drops against sliding ledge cycles and mass momentum management. It tests your strategy in building coin piles without spilling into side gutters.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Drop your coins when the sliding shelf is fully extended backward to maximize forward pushing leverage.
  • 2. Target clusters with loose coins teetering over the front edge rather than dropping into empty spaces.
  • 3. Avoid creating coin "cliffs" (bridged overlaps) that absorb pushes without advancing forward.
  • 4. Aim for heavy prize bars and gem tokens that push large clusters of loose silver off the shelf.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do coins that fall into side gutters count toward your score?

No, side gutters are house drains; only coins and tokens pushed cleanly over the front edge credit your score.

How many coins do you start with in Arcade Coin Pusher?

You start with 40 coins in your tray, earning one free coin recharge every ten seconds of gameplay.

Can you tilt or shake the coin pusher machine?

You can tap the machine nudge button up to three times per session, with excess shaking locking the mechanism.
