Mini Reversi 6x6

Flank your opponent’s discs between your own to flip them to your color on a compact 6x6 board.

🎯 Objective

Outflank opposing discs between your own to flip them over, finishing the match with majority board control.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Place your disc on an empty square such that you trap one or more opponent discs between your new piece and another of your pieces. All trapped discs flip to your color.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This board game measures boundary stability evaluation and positional sacrifice strategy. It evaluates your control of stable corner anchors versus temporary disc counts.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Capture corner squares at all costs; corner discs can never be flipped over by your opponent.
  • 2. Avoid playing adjacent to open corners (the "C-squares" and "X-squares") unless you are certain to capture the corner next turn.
  • 3. Minimize early disc flips; having fewer discs in the mid-game leaves your opponent with fewer legal move options.
  • 4. Control stable edges that connect directly into secured corners.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What grid size is used in Mini Reversi?

This compact version uses a 6x6 grid (36 squares) for faster, high-intensity tactical rounds.

What happens if a player has no legal moves?

If you have no moves that outflank at least one opposing disc, your turn passes automatically to your opponent.

How does the game end in Mini Reversi?

The game concludes when all 36 squares are filled or when neither player can make a legal move.
