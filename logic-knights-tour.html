🎯 Objective

Guide the chess knight to visit as many squares on the board as possible without landing on any square twice.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Click any valid L-shaped knight move (2 squares along one axis, 1 square perpendicular). Visit as many distinct chessboard squares as possible. Can you touch all 25 squares?

Scored in streak (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This classic chess problem measures graph traversal and constraint satisfaction. It tests your application of Warnsdorff heuristic rules across L-shaped knight moves.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Apply Warnsdorff rule: always move the knight to the square with the fewest remaining legal exits.
  • 2. Tour outer board perimeter squares and corners early, as they are the hardest to reach later.
  • 3. Avoid jumping toward the board center early, as central squares provide vital transit routes for the endgame.
  • 4. Look two moves ahead to ensure your destination square does not leave your knight completely stranded.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a full Knight Tour?

A full tour visits every single square on the board (e.g. all 64 squares on an 8x8 board) exactly once.

How does the chess knight move?

The knight moves in an "L-shape": two squares in one cardinal direction followed by one square perpendicular.

What grid sizes are available in Knight Leap Puzzle?

You can start on a smaller 5x5 board (25 squares) to learn the heuristic before graduating to a full 8x8 chessboard.
