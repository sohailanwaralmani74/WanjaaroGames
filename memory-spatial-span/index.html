🎯 Objective

Memorize the positions of flashed numbers across the grid, then click their hidden locations in ascending order (1 to 9).

📋 How to Play & Scoring

A set of tiles will glow for 1.5 seconds. Once they turn blank, tap the exact tiles that were highlighted. The grid size and count increase as your level rises.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This test measures working memory capacity under rapid visual masking. It evaluates how many simultaneous spatial coordinates you can encode in a fraction of a second.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Group numbers into geometric shapes like triangles or lines to memorize clusters at once.
  • 2. Anchor your eyes on numbers 1, 2, and 3 first, as tapping these quickly gives you time to recall later positions.
  • 3. Do not try to memorize all positions at once; trace an imaginary path from 1 through the highest number.
  • 4. Breathe steadily and stay calm during the brief flash interval before cards mask into white blocks.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why is this challenge named after the chimpanzee Ayumu?

At Kyoto University, a young chimpanzee named Ayumu routinely recalled all 9 masked numbers accurately in under 0.7 seconds, outperforming human university students.

What is the average human score on the spatial span test?

Most adults consistently remember 7 to 9 numbers, aligning with standard working memory limits.

Does the flash duration change as levels progress?

Yes, higher difficulty stages reduce display exposure time from 1000 milliseconds down to 300 milliseconds.
