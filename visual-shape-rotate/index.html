🎯 Objective

Determine whether a rotated 3D polygon is an exact rotation or a mirrored reflection of the target shape.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Two isometric shapes are shown at different angular orientations. Decide if they are the exact same shape merely rotated in 3D space, or if they are inverted chiral mirror images.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This test measures mental rotation capability and 3D spatial transformation speed. It benchmarks how accurately you mentally manipulate three-dimensional objects in space.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Pick one distinct protruding feature or colored face and mentally track where it would land after rotation.
  • 2. Check the handedness of corners: if a point turns clockwise on the original, it must remain clockwise on a true rotation.
  • 3. Use keyboard shortcut keys (Left for "Mirrored", Right for "Rotated") for fastest response times.
  • 4. Do not try to rotate the entire object at once; focus on two connected perpendicular edges.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is mental rotation in spatial cognitive psychology?

First quantified by Shepard and Metzler in 1971, mental rotation describes the internal process of rotating 2D or 3D mental representations.

What rotation angles are tested in this challenge?

Angles range from 45° to 315° across all three rotational axes (yaw, pitch, and roll).

How does response time correlate with rotation angle?

Response latency typically increases linearly with the degrees of rotation needed to align the shapes.
