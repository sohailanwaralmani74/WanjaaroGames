🎯 Objective

Acquire and eliminate shrinking target nodes across the arena before they collapse to zero radius.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Click or tap moving bullseyes before they collapse to zero size. Hitting closer to the dead center yields maximum points (up to 100 per target). Complete 15 targets.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This trainer measures ballistic cursor flick accuracy and fine cursor deceleration. It benchmarks how quickly and accurately you reposition your crosshair onto variable-sized targets.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Lower your mouse DPI sensitivity if you notice your cursor overshooting past small targets.
  • 2. Flick your wrist directly toward the target center, then decelerate into a micro-correction.
  • 3. Target shrinking discs first before they expire to preserve your consecutive streak multiplier.
  • 4. Keep your arm relaxed and glide your mouse across a consistent, clean mousepad surface.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do targets shrink at the same rate throughout the drill?

Targets shrink faster as your consecutive score streak increases, demanding faster flick timing.

What is the optimal mouse sensitivity for Precision Sniper?

A medium sensitivity where one comfortable wrist swipe covers approximately half your screen width offers the best balance.

How does miss penalty affect the score?

Clicking empty arena space deducts accuracy percentage and resets your active combo streak.
