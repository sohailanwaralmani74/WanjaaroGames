📋 How to Play & Scoring

Click or tap bubbles rising from bottom to top. Pop as many as possible within 30 seconds. Golden bubbles trigger 2x double point frenzy!

🧠 What This Game Measures

This arcade game measures multi-target visual tracking and rapid spatial pointing accuracy. It evaluates how quickly you acquire and click moving floating targets.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Prioritize bubbles near the top of the screen before they float away and escape.
  • 2. Target bubble clusters to chain rapid sequential clicks with minimal cursor travel.
  • 3. Pop golden bonus bubbles immediately for double-point bursts.
  • 4. Avoid clicking red spiky hazard bubbles that deduct ten points and freeze your cursor momentarily.
