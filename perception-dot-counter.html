🎯 Objective

Estimate or count the total number of dots flashed on screen for a split second before entering your count.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Dots flash on the screen for exactly 500 milliseconds and then vanish. Select the exact count from the multiple-choice options. Tests pure visual subitizing.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This test measures rapid visual subitizing and spatial quantity estimation. It benchmarks your capacity to perceive small group quantities instantly without counting one by one.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. For quantities under 5, rely on instant pattern recognition (subitizing) rather than counting.
  • 2. For larger clusters, divide the screen mentally into halves and estimate the two portions.
  • 3. Trust your immediate impression instead of second-guessing after the dots vanish.
  • 4. Notice geometric groupings like triangles or clusters of three to speed up mental tallying.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is "subitizing" in visual perception?

Subitizing is the rapid, accurate, and confident judgment of numbers performed instantly for small quantities (typically 1 to 4 items).

How long do the dots stay visible on screen?

Dots are displayed for 300 to 500 milliseconds before turning blank to prevent manual finger counting.

Is partial credit given for near guesses?

Exact matches award full points, with partial points granted if your estimate is within plus-or-minus one dot.
