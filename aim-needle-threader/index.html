🎯 Objective

Steer a continuous thread line through alternating narrow needle eyes moving in opposite directions.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Hold spacebar or touch screen to raise the thread line; release to lower it. Thread the line cleanly through the eyes of the moving needles as they scroll by.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This game measures horizontal precision tracking and rhythmic clearance timing. It tests your ability to guide a continuous trace through moving spatial bottlenecks.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Focus on the upcoming eye three seconds before your thread reaches its opening.
  • 2. Make vertical adjustments early so the thread enters horizontally flat rather than at a steep angle.
  • 3. Do not over-correct if you enter slightly off-center; gentle steering prevents perimeter grazing.
  • 4. Keep your eyes on the needle eye center rather than looking back at the completed thread path.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if the thread touches the metal needle eye?

Grazing the needle edge snaps the thread and ends the round immediately.

Do the needle eyes change in width as you progress?

Yes, the eye openings narrow progressively as your score crosses milestones at 10, 25, and 50 needles.

Can the thread speed be paused or slowed down?

Thread forward velocity is constant, requiring real-time steering adjustments without pausing.
