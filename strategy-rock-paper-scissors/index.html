💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. People frequently repeat winning throws and cycle forward after losing throws.
  • 2. Track your opponent's past three choices to anticipate whether they play in cyclic (Rock-Paper-Scissors) order.
  • 3. Break your own predictable rhythms by introducing intentional counter-intuitive choices.
  • 4. Observe whether the AI shifts strategies after falling two points behind.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does the AI use a true random number generator in this game?

The Predictive Master AI uses a Markov transition model that tracks your history and predicts your next throw based on past habits.

How many rounds make up a complete match?

Matches are played as best of 15 rounds, displaying live win-loss-tie percentages throughout.

What happens on a tie?

Ties award zero points to both players and advance the round counter without breaking win streaks.
