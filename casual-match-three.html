Swap adjacent shimmering gems to form horizontal or vertical lines of 3 or more.

🎯 Objective

Swap adjacent gems on the grid to create lines of three or more matching jewels, triggering cascade combos.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Click or drag adjacent gems to swap their positions. Forming a line of 3 or more identical gems clears them, causing new gems to cascade downward. Score as much as possible in 60s.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This casual puzzle measures multi-directional pattern recognition and cascade combo forecasting. It benchmarks your efficiency at spotting high-value match opportunities across dense grids.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Make matches near the bottom of the board to trigger rising cascade reactions that clear upper gems for free.
  • 2. Create 4-gem lines to forge explosive stripe gems, and 5-gem lines to craft rainbow hyper-cubes.
  • 3. Combine two special gems together (like a stripe gem plus a bomb gem) for massive screen-clearing blasts.
  • 4. Scan for vertical matches as well as horizontal ones to prevent single-direction tunnel vision.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when you match five gems in an L-shape or T-shape?

Matching five gems in a cross, L, or T shape creates an explosive jewel bomb that detonates a 3x3 radius.

Is there a move limit or time limit in Jewel Blitz Swap?

You can choose between a 60-second time rush mode and a relaxed 30-move tactical challenge mode.

What happens if no valid moves remain on the board?

If no legal matches exist, the board automatically shuffles all gems without penalty.
