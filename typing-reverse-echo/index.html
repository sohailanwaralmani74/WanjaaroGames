🎯 Objective

Read words displayed on screen and type their letters backward in exact reverse order.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

A word appears on screen. Type its letters in reverse order from right to left as fast as possible. Tests your brain's ability to invert mental phonemes.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This drill measures mental spelling inversion and lexical manipulation. It challenges your ability to break apart a word and reverse its character order before typing.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Read the word from right to left in your mind before striking the first key.
  • 2. Spell out suffixes first (for example, typing "g-n-i" for words ending in "-ing").
  • 3. Do not rush; one wrong letter ruins the reverse sequence and requires backspacing.
  • 4. Whisper the reversed letter sounds to yourself to maintain character order.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is an example of Reverse Typist gameplay?

If the prompt shows "PLANET", you must immediately type "TENALP" followed by the Enter or Space key.

Does word length increase on higher levels?

Rounds begin with 3-letter words and progressively advance to complex 8-letter vocabulary.

How does Reverse Typist score performance?

Points are based on words correctly inverted per minute along with accuracy percentages.
