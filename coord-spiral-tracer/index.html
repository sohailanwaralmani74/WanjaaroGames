🎯 Objective

Trace a cursor or stylus continuously along a winding Archimedean spiral without straying outside the boundaries.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Click and drag your cursor or finger along the spiral guide line. Stay within the track boundaries. Speed and precision are scored together.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This test measures continuous curve tracking speed and fine motor contour tracing. It records spatial path error and deviation under continuous angular travel.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Rest your wrist lightly on the surface to maintain smooth, continuous circular pivoting.
  • 2. Maintain a moderate, steady tracking speed rather than speeding up then stopping.
  • 3. Look slightly ahead of your cursor to anticipate the increasing radius of outer spiral loops.
  • 4. Use a stylus or mouse with a clean optical sensor for optimal continuous path fidelity.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is deviation measured in Spiral Curve Tracer?

The engine records the pixel distance between your cursor and the mathematical center-line of the spiral path.

Does the spiral path widen or narrow as you progress?

The path corridor narrows from 40 pixels on early loops down to 15 pixels on outer rings.

Is tracing time counted in the final score?

Yes, your score combines total path completion percentage with elapsed completion time.
