🎯 Objective

Sort falling colored or patterned items into their corresponding left and right sorting bins at high speed.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

Objects fall rapidly. Press Left Arrow / Left Button for Blue, Down for Red, Right for Yellow. Wrong sorts lose lives. Survive 60 seconds of accelerating sorting.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This game measures rapid visual classification and dual-choice motor execution. It benchmarks your categorization decision speed under continuous arrival rates.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

Scores and personal records are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This site may use cookies for advertisements and analytics as described in the Privacy Policy.

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. Focus on a single distinguishing attribute (like color or shape) to classify items instantaneously.
  • 2. Use both hands on keyboard shortcuts (Z for left bin, M for right bin) for the fastest sorting rate.
  • 3. Anticipate the next item in the conveyor queue while sorting the active item.
  • 4. Do not guess; misplacing an item into the wrong bin breaks your bonus multiplier.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How fast do items arrive on the sorting conveyor?

Item frequency ramps up every ten successful sorts, challenging your visual classification limits.

Can you sort items with touch gestures on mobile?

Yes, simply swipe items toward the left or right bin, or tap the large on-screen sorting zone buttons.

How long does a standard Bin Sort Rush round last?

Standard sessions last 45 seconds of continuous sorting action.
