🎯 Objective

Plot the exact Cartesian coordinates of a point after reflecting it across a diagonal or cardinal mirror line.

📋 How to Play & Scoring

A point is marked on a coordinate grid alongside a diagonal mirror line (y = x). Tap the exact grid coordinate where the point will appear after reflection across the mirror.

Scored in pts (higher scores indicate better performance).

🧠 What This Game Measures

This spatial drill measures Cartesian coordinate transformation and reflection geometry. It tests your calculation of mirrored coordinate pairs (x, y) across varying reflection axes.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

💡 Strategy & Gameplay Tips

  • 1. When reflecting over the vertical y-axis, invert the sign of the x-coordinate: (x, y) becomes (-x, y).
  • 2. When reflecting over the horizontal x-axis, invert the sign of the y-coordinate: (x, y) becomes (x, -y).
  • 3. When reflecting over the diagonal line y = x, swap the coordinates completely: (x, y) becomes (y, x).
  • 4. Measure the perpendicular distance from the point to the mirror line; the reflection lies at the exact same distance on the opposite side.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What coordinate axes are featured in Mirror Coordinate Plotter?

Problems feature standard Cartesian grids with reflections across x=0, y=0, y=x, and offset parallel lines.

How do you submit your answer?

Click directly on the destination grid intersection point, or type the coordinates into the input box.

What happens if you click one grid unit off?

The system highlights your offset error in red and displays the correct mathematical reflection line.
